Energet

Energet helps find homeowners and businesses who may benefit from looking at their energy costs, solar or batteries.

What Energet does

Contacts potential customersThey use phone, email and SMS outreach to speak with people about their energy situation.
Checks whether help may be usefulThey ask simple questions about the property, power use and interest in an energy review.
Books the appointmentIf a customer wants help, Energet can arrange a time for me to speak with them.
Helps collect useful informationWhere possible, they collect an electricity bill before the meeting so I have a better starting point.
No need to already be shopping for solarThe aim is to help people who may have an energy problem but have not yet had time to look into it.

Energet is mainly part of the outreach and appointment process. They are not the installer in the normal Energy With Mark customer journey.

My role

I stay as your point of contact.

My job is to help you understand the numbers, compare the useful options and explain what happens next.

If a project moves forward, I help connect the advice stage with the company supplying and installing the system.

You will know who you are dealing with.
Before you agree to a project, the quote or proposal should clearly show the company responsible for supplying and installing the system.
How am I paid?

My advice is free to the customer.

If a system is purchased through a supplier or installer I work with, that company pays me. There is no separate advice fee from me.
