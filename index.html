The simplest useful first step

Have an electricity bill? Start there.

A recent bill can show how much electricity you use, what you pay, solar exports, tariffs and — for some businesses — demand charges. It gives us facts to work from instead of assumptions.

Not every answer is “buy more”

The right outcome depends on the numbers.

Sometimes solar is the strongest next step. Sometimes a battery solves a real problem. Sometimes the existing system is already doing what it should.

Solar may make sense

When useful daytime generation can replace grid electricity and the site supports it.

A battery may make sense

When useful solar surplus can replace expensive later grid use or solve another clear need.

No change may make sense

If the numbers do not support another investment, that should be visible too.
