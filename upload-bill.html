What happens after you upload

I review the information before deciding what comes next.

1. Your bill enters a private review areaIt is not automatically turned into a customer or assessment record.
2. I look for the useful energy informationUsage, rates, exports, charges and obvious gaps.
3. I identify what is still neededIf site details, inverter information or other data would improve the assessment, I can request it.
What the bill can tell us

A useful first look at your current energy position.

Electricity usage

How much electricity was used over the billing period.

Rates and tariff structure

What you are paying for grid electricity and how the tariff is structured.

Solar exports where shown

Useful when reviewing an existing solar system or the case for battery storage.

Commercial charges where relevant

Supply charges and demand charges can materially change a business energy assessment.

What information is still missing

The bill does not answer every question. It helps identify the next useful information to collect.
