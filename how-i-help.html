About Mark Fitzpatrick

I work with Australian homeowners, businesses, property owners, sporting clubs and community organisations considering solar, battery storage and other renewable-energy solutions.

My approach is based on understanding the customer's situation before recommending a system. I have more than 30 years of experience working with Australian businesses, and I focus on making the technical and financial decision easier to understand.

I do not believe every customer needs the same solution

There are situations where I may recommend solar without a battery, battery storage, an expansion to an existing system, or no change for now. The recommendation should follow the evidence.

Who completes the installation?

I manage the customer discussion, assessment, proposal and ongoing relationship. Once a project is approved, the physical installation is completed by the appropriate qualified installation partner. I remain involved before, during and after that handover.
