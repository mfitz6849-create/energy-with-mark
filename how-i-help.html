The process

Understand → Recommend → Deliver → Support

The aim is to give you enough clarity to make a sensible decision, then keep the handover into delivery understandable.

1
Understand

Discuss your goals and review the bill, usage, site and existing-system information that matters.

2
Recommend

Assess suitable options and prepare a proposal explaining pricing, expected benefits and important assumptions.

3
Deliver

Once you approve a project, it moves to the appropriate qualified installation partner for physical installation.

4
Support

I remain involved in the customer relationship, follow up after installation and help keep questions or issues visible until they reach the right people.

What I will not assume

Not every customer needs the same solution.

There are situations where solar without a battery is stronger, where storage has a useful job to do, where an existing system should be expanded or replaced, and where doing nothing for now is the sensible result.

The recommendation should follow the evidence.
The goal is not to sell the largest system. It is to make the practical and financial trade-offs clear enough for you to decide.
What you can expect

A clearer decision, not more jargon.

Clear explanations

Technical and financial information explained in plain language.

Realistic expectations

Savings are estimates. The assumptions behind those estimates should be visible.

Useful comparisons

Where it helps, compare different approaches rather than forcing a single system choice.
