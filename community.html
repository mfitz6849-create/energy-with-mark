Whole-site view

Lighting, hot water, pumps, EV charging and other energy upgrades can change what the best solar or battery design looks like.

Usage can be very different from a normal business

A sporting club may have low daytime use but heavy evening demand from lighting, kitchens, bars, change rooms and events. Another community facility may run refrigeration, offices or pumps throughout the day. The same solar design will not suit both sites.

Who owns the site matters

Some clubs own their buildings. Others operate from council, government, school or privately owned property. Before a project moves forward, the organisation may need approval from the property owner or another responsible body.

This should be identified early so the project is designed around the actual site arrangements rather than discovered after the proposal is prepared.

What I look at

  • Recent electricity bills
  • When the facility is used
  • Daytime and evening loads
  • Sports lighting and other large equipment
  • Refrigeration, kitchens, hot water and pumps
  • Existing solar and exports
  • Future EV charging or electrification
  • Property ownership and approval requirements
  • Project budget and available funding options

Funding and grants need to be checked at the time

Government and community funding programs can change. I do not treat an old grant announcement as guaranteed funding. Where a current program may apply, eligibility, timing and project requirements should be checked against the current official rules before relying on it.

Start with the project, not the grant.
A good project should first make sense for the facility. Funding can then improve an already useful project rather than being the only reason it exists.

What happens if the project proceeds?

I can help with the customer discussion, energy assessment, proposal explanation and project coordination. The proposal or agreement should identify the company responsible for the commercial project, and qualified installation professionals complete the physical installation.
