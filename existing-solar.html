Why can bills still be high?

Common reasons

🌙

You use more power at night

Solar stops after sunset, but your home or business may still use a lot of power.

↗

Your power use has grown

New appliances, air conditioning, EVs or business equipment can raise your power use.

☀

Your solar may be too small or old

An older system may not match the amount of power you use today.
