Common reasons

Why can electricity bills still be high with solar?

Solar can be working correctly while the overall energy picture has changed.

More evening or overnight use

Solar generation may be strong during the day while the home or business still buys substantial electricity later.

The system is now too small

Energy use may have increased since the system was installed.

Large daytime exports

Exporting solar for a low feed-in value can leave an opportunity to use more of that energy on site.

New loads or changed behaviour

EVs, pools, air conditioning, heating, machinery or occupancy changes can materially increase consumption.

Age or performance issues

An older system may need its actual performance and equipment limits checked before an upgrade is recommended.

Future needs

EV charging and backup can change the answer.

A future EV may justify more generation or change the best time to use energy. Backup is a separate design question because keeping energy in reserve for outages can change how a battery is used day to day.

Start with the system you already own.
You do not need to decide that you want a particular battery size before the assessment. The useful size and design should follow the evidence.
