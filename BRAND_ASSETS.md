# Energy With Mark — Approved Brand Assets

## Master brand

Energy With Mark Brand v1.0 is the default public brand identity.

- Primary name: **Energy With Mark**
- Descriptor: **Solar • Batteries • Energy Advice**
- Supporting line: **Clear Advice • Smart Solutions • Better Energy Futures**
- Core colours: Deep Teal `#0F2E2E`, Green Teal `#1B4B46`, Energy Lime `#C6E11A`, Soft Off-White `#F1F6F3`, White `#FFFFFF`
- Typography: Montserrat preferred, with a clean modern sans-serif fallback
- Logo: simple circular/orbit-style EW mark with centred, easy-to-read EW lettering

## Portrait usage rules

- The site is a personal brand, so Mark's image should be visible at useful trust points across the public website.
- Prefer the primary portrait when only one image is needed.
- Use both portraits across the site rather than repeating the same pose everywhere.
- Do not heavily stylise, cartoonise, reshape or change Mark's appearance without explicit approval.
- Keep transparent-background versions as the master website assets.
- Place portraits on clean Energy With Mark backgrounds using the approved teal, lime and off-white palette.
- Do not let portraits compete with the main call to action or calculator; they should add trust and personal connection.
