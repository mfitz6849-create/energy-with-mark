How I work out what size system a business needs

I do not start by asking how many panels will fit on the roof. I start with the business.

Energy profile

  • Annual electricity use
  • Daytime consumption
  • Operating hours
  • Seasonal changes
  • Demand charges
  • Solar exports

Future requirements

  • New machinery
  • Business expansion
  • EV charging
  • Electrification
  • Battery storage
  • Network limits

Commercial batteries should solve a real problem

A battery may be worth investigating when the business exports useful solar during the day and then buys expensive electricity later. It may also help with certain demand-charge patterns or backup requirements.

The battery question is not “Should my business have one?”
It is “What problem will the battery solve, and is the benefit worth the cost?”

Understand the financial result

A proposal can include expected generation, self-consumption, exports, estimated annual savings, payback, longer-term savings, incentives and important assumptions. Larger projects may also benefit from NPV or IRR analysis.

Your electricity bill is the starting point

A commercial bill may show consumption, rates, demand charges, supply charges and existing solar exports. For more complex projects, interval data can show when the business uses electricity throughout the day.
