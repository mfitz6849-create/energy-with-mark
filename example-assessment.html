3. What still needs checking

The assessment separates facts from assumptions.

◷

When you use power

Daytime versus evening use can materially change the value of solar and batteries.

⌂

Roof and site

Roof space, shade, orientation, switchboard and meter details need to be confirmed.

▣

Battery job

A battery should solve a real problem, not simply be added because it is available.

$

Final pricing

Example conclusion

Solar appears worth investigating further. A battery may also be useful, but it should not be recommended until the household’s usage timing and likely solar exports are known. The next step is to model the real bill and confirm the property details.
