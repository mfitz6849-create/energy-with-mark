How we move forward

Simple steps to get the right solar solution.

◎
1Confirm normal operating hours and site use

2Check roof, switchboard and phase

Confirm the physical site can support the design.

3Review interval data and export approval

Use extra data where it improves the decision.

4Model two sensible solar options

Compare a standard and larger case.

5Review savings and choose the best fit

Proceed only if the numbers stack up.
