What happens next

Here is how we work out the sensible path.

☀⌂
1Check the current solar system

Confirm health and output.

2Confirm panel and inverter details

Record model, age and size.

3Review home usage and future loads

Understand what the battery would need to do.

4Compare battery vs upgrade options

Test retention against replacement.

5Show simple savings and payback numbers

Keep a no-change option.

?What we still need to confirm

✓Inverter brand / model
✓Monitoring / performance

PATH C

No change yet if the numbers do not stack up
