Privacy & transparency

Privacy Notice

How information submitted through this advisory website and ordinary website-usage information are intended to be handled.

Launch note: This notice is a practical working notice for the current website. It will be reviewed again before live customer uploads and CRM submissions are enabled, including the final private database, shared company records, installer access and retention arrangements.

Information that may be collected

Depending on what you choose to provide, information may include your name, contact details, property or business information, electricity bills, energy-use information, existing solar or battery details, photos, appointment preferences and information about what you want to achieve.

Why the information is collected

The information is intended to be used to understand your enquiry, communicate with you, assess solar or battery options, prepare or explain proposals, manage follow-up and, if you decide to proceed, coordinate relevant project and installation information.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the public website is used. This can include page views, general device and browser information, referral source, broad location information and interactions such as clicking assessment, bill-upload, booking or calculator links. The website is configured so customer names, email addresses, electricity-bill contents, uploaded files and form-field contents are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to provide website measurement. Advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled in the site configuration. Analytics information is used to improve the website, understand which information is useful and measure which public content leads people towards an enquiry.

Electricity bills and uploaded files

Electricity bills and site photos can contain personal, account, property or energy-use information. Only provide information that is relevant to your enquiry. The live system will be configured so uploaded files are stored in controlled business systems with access limited to people who need the information for the enquiry or project.

Installation partners, represented companies and other service providers

If you decide to proceed, relevant project information may need to be provided to the company responsible for the project, qualified installation partners or other service providers involved in delivering the approved work. The final live-data arrangements will separate Mark's private master records from controlled company-accessible copies and will limit shared information to what is needed for the relevant project.

Automated assistance

Software may assist with organising submitted information, identifying missing information, creating internal summaries, generating tasks or preparing drafts. Final customer-specific recommendations, system sizing, pricing decisions and sensitive customer communication remain under adviser control.

Access, correction and questions

For questions about information you have provided, or to request access or correction where applicable, contact Mark Fitzpatrick at mark.fitzpatrick@classaenergy.com.au or 0434 151 237.

Retention and security

Before live customer submissions are enabled, a retention period, deletion process, access-control process and incident-response process will be confirmed for customer bills, photos, CRM records and project files.

Updates

This notice will be reviewed whenever the website, CRM, analytics, AI/automation, installation-partner process or information-handling arrangements materially change.

Principle: collect what is useful for the customer’s enquiry, explain why it is being collected, protect it appropriately and keep human responsibility for the final recommendation.
